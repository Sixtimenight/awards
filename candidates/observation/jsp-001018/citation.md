# Citation

Candidate under observation; verification and review pending.

## English

Norbert Hegyvari proved that a strictly increasing finite sequence of positive integers with bounded gaps cannot have all consecutive-interval sums distinct once its last term is sufficiently large (Acta Math. Hungar. 48 (1986), 193-200). A machine-checked formal proof in Lean 4 has been completed with an explicit finite threshold. This record represents a candidate submission currently pending formal verification and curator review.
