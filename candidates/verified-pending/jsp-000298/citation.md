## English

Erdős Problem JSP-000298 (Erdős Problem #360) investigates the minimum number of colors $f(n)$ required to color the integers $\{1, \dots, n-1\}$ such that no monochromatic subset sums to $n$. Originally posed by Erdős and Graham (1980), bounds of order $n^{1/3 \pm o(1)}$ were established by Noga Alon and Paul Erdős (*Acta Arithmetica* 74(3), 1996, pp. 269–272), with further sharp order bounds established by Conlon, Fox, and Pham (2021).

This machine-checked Lean 4 formalization proves both the elementary cubic-root bound, the general sieve-remainder upper bound theorem from Alon–Erdős (1996, Section 2), and connects the remainder to Mathlib's Selberg sieve framework:
1. **Sieve-Remainder Bound**: For any $n \ge 2, s \ge 1$, and finite set $P$ with elements not dividing $n$, there exists an explicit monochromatic subset-sum-avoiding coloring using $s + |P| + \lceil |R| / s \rceil$ colors (`exists_coloring_sieve`, `minColors_le_sieve`), where $R = \{x \in \{1, \dots, n-1\} \mid (s+1)x < n \wedge \forall p \in P, p \nmid x\}$. Crucially, this combinatorial theorem does not require $n \le s^3$.
2. **Prime Set Specialization**: Specializing to $P = \{p \le s \mid p.\text{Prime} \wedge p \nmid n\}$ yields $f(n) \le 2s + \lceil |R| / s \rceil$ (`minColors_le_two_s_add_remainder`), with $R$ equivalent to a coprimality filter on $\{1, \dots, \lfloor(n-1)/(s+1)\rfloor\}$ (`sieve_remainder_eq_coprime_filter`).
3. **Finite Selberg Sieve Bound**: Optimization of the diagonalized quadratic form on canonical normalized weights $w(d)$ supported on $d \le z$ proves unconditionally that $\text{mainSum}(\lambda^2 w) = 1/G$ (`mainSum_selbergWeight_eq`), $\text{errSum}(\lambda^2 w) \le z^4$ (`errSum_selbergWeight_le`), and deduces the effective finite bound $|R| \le \lfloor(n-1)/(s+1)\rfloor / G + z^4$ (`selberg_remainder_bound`), where $G = \sum_{d \in D.\text{divisors}, d \le z} \text{selbergTerms}(d) \ge 1$ (`sieveG_ge_one`).
4. **Prime Reciprocal Sum Lower Bounds & Quantitative Bounds**: Evaluates prime Selberg terms $\text{selbergTerms}(p) = 1/(p-1)$ (`selbergTerms_prime`), lower bounds $G \ge 1 + \sum_{p \in P, p \le z} \frac{1}{p - 1}$ (`sieveG_ge_one_add_sum_primes`), and deduces explicit quantitative chromatic bounds $f(n) \le 2s + \lceil \frac{n - 1}{s(s+1) g_0} + \frac{z^4}{s} \rceil$ (`minColors_le_of_sieveG_lower_bound`), yielding the full prime reciprocal sum bound (`minColors_le_of_sum_primes`) and prime subset bounds (`minColors_le_of_prime_subset`).
5. **Mathlib Selberg Sieve Instantiation**: Direct connection to `Mathlib.NumberTheory.SelbergSieve.BoundingSieve` (`erdosBoundingSieve`), proving the sifted sum equals $|R|$ (`siftedSum_eq_card_remainder`), deriving the upper Moebius bound $|R| \le m \cdot \text{mainSum}(\mu^+) + \text{errSum}(\mu^+)$ (`card_remainder_le_mainSum_errSum`), and establishing the controlled error bound $|\text{rem}(d)| \le 1$ for all divisors $d \mid D$ (`erdos_rem_bound_of_mem_divisors`).
6. **Cubic-Root Baseline**: For any $n \le s^3$, an explicit $2s$-coloring avoids monochromatic subset sums (`exists_coloring_of_le_cube`, `minColors_le_two_mul_s`), yielding $f(n) \le 2\lceil n^{1/3}\rceil$.
7. **Covering Principle & Avoidance Blocks**: General covering theorem (`exists_coloring_of_avoiding_family`), interval blocks for any $k \ge 1$ (`interval_block_avoids_subset_sum`), modular divisibility avoidance (`dvd_subset_sum_free`), and small-cardinality blocks (`small_card_block_avoids_subset_sum`).
8. **Lower Bound**: $f(n) \ge 2$ for all $n \ge 3$ (`minColors_ge_two`).
9. **Asymptotic Reduction & Conlon–Fox–Pham (2021)**: Formal asymptotic growth witnesses (`HasChromaticUpperBound`, `HasChromaticLowerBound`) and the two-sided asymptotic growth reduction theorem (`conlon_fox_pham_bounds`).
