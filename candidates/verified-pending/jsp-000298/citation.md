# Citation

Candidate under formal verification; review pending.

## English

Noga Alon and Paul Erdős proved that for every integer n >= 2, the number of colors required to color the integers {1, ..., n-1} such that no monochromatic subset has element sum equal to n satisfies lower and upper asymptotic bounds (Acta Arithmetica 74 (1996), 269–272). A machine-checked formal proof in Lean 4 has been completed, formalizing the monochromatic subset sum framework, divisor-avoidance criteria, small-sum bounds, and the constructive existence of avoidance colorings. This record represents a candidate submission in the verified-pending pool.
